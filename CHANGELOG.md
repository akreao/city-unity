# Changelog

Each `## <version>` section is that version's release notes: the release
workflow publishes it as the GitHub Release text, which the Ragnarok Offline
app shows players under "What's new". Write it for players.

## 0.1.1
- First release: kRO's Major City Contribution system.
- Join the City Merchant Union Manager in the Eden Group lobby, then find
  Union Insignias on the monsters you hunt. Party members in the union who are
  close by can find them too.
- Invest insignias once a day with the Investment Officer of Prontera, Geffen,
  Payon, Morroc, Juno, Einbroch, Lighthalzen, Rachel or Veins.
- Each city's Merchant Union sells its Unity gear as your contribution grows:
  weapons and shields, then shoes, armor and garment. The armor gives +4% EXP
  up to base level 100.
- The City Merchant Union Board posts a weekly wanted boss and daily A, B and
  C-rank deliveries to NPCs inside dungeons, paid in insignias by the
  Secretary Governor.
- English quest text for every union quest.
- Settings: insignia drop chance and a contribution multiplier.
