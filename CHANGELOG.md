# Changelog

Each `## <version>` section is that version's release notes: the release
workflow publishes it as the GitHub Release text, which the Ragnarok Offline
app shows players under "What's new". Write it for players.

## 0.1.4
- The Unity Mace and Unity Two-Handed Axe tooltips now list Druid among
  the jobs that can equip them. Druids could already use both; the English
  text just left them out. Restart the app after updating so the client
  picks up the new text.

## 0.1.3
- The City Merchant Union Board is back where kRO has it: the chalkboard on
  the wall behind the Union Manager in the Eden Group lobby. Like on kRO, the
  board itself is invisible, so you click the chalkboard to read the wanted
  bounties and deliveries.

## 0.1.2
- Works alongside World Sync Extras. That mod has its own City Investment,
  built on the same quests, insignias and NPC spots, so the two used to stand
  on top of each other. While World Sync Extras' "City Investment" setting is
  ticked (it is by default), City Unity now steps aside: its NPCs and insignia
  drops are off. To play City Unity instead, untick City Investment in World
  Sync Extras' settings and restart the server.
- Each mod keeps its own city contribution, so switching between them doesn't
  carry it over.

## 0.1.1
- First release: kRO's Major City Contribution system.
- Join the City Merchant Union Manager in the Eden Group lobby, then find
  Union Insignias on the monsters you hunt. Party members in the union who are
  close by can find them too.
- Invest insignias once a day with the Investment Officer of Prontera, Geffen,
  Payon, Morroc, Juno, Einbroch, Lighthalzen, Rachel or Veins.
- Each city's Merchant Union sells its Unity gear as your contribution grows:
  weapons and shields, then shoes, armor and garment. The armor gives +4% EXP
  up to base level 100.
- The City Merchant Union Board posts a weekly wanted boss and daily A, B and
  C-rank deliveries to NPCs inside dungeons, paid in insignias by the
  Secretary Governor.
- English quest text for every union quest.
- Settings: insignia drop chance and a contribution multiplier.
